# CPU 

For the CPU i took AMD’s Ryzen 9 9950X. for specs, it have  has 16 cores/32 threads, boosts up to 5.7 GHz, runs on AM5 with PCIe 5.0, and is specced at 170 W. 

it’s apparently built for heavy multitasking and creation work, and it’s more than fast enough for modern games. 

A integrated amd module also allows to mutualize some part of the workload with AMD GPU's, but this feature isnt enable with other card brands like nvidia.

# GPU: 

For the GPU i've choosen NVIDIA’s GeForce RTX 5090. it’s based on the Blackwell architecture with 32 GB of GDDR7 and is aimed at 4K gaming with full ray tracing plus big AI/creator workloads. 

On the gaming side, this gpu is also known to have a increased framerate, due to a in built AI mudule that predict and simulate frames.

# Reflection 

the cpu handles the “brains” of the system (logic, background tasks, loading, compile/render setup), while the gpu does the massive parallel work (raster, ray tracing, AI). 

at 1080p the cpu can matter more, at 4K the gpu is usually the limiter, and a 5090-class card pulls far ahead. 

pairing a 9950X with a 5090 gives in my opinion top tier performance for whatever domestic task the PC is intended for. Those components are also quite pricy.
